What goes with growth? (rank correlations across 64 districts)
==============================================================
Density in 1991 vs growth 1991-2022 (Spearman): -0.00 (p=0.995)
District size in 1991 vs growth 1991-2022:       +0.14 (p=0.254)
Year established vs growth 1991-2022:            -0.20 (p=0.105)
Log-log slope, area vs 2022 population:           0.43 (r=0.36) - population rises less than proportionally with area.
Interpretation: NONE of these is statistically significant (all p > 0.10). How dense a district was in 1991 told us
nothing about how fast it grew (rho ~ 0.00); size and age of district are also weak predictors.
Growth is driven by specific places (the Dhaka belt, the south-eastern hills and coast, Sylhet) rather than by a
simple density or age rule. Correlation is not causation.
Growth in 2011-2022 fell below 2001-2011 in 44 of 64 districts, and below 1991-2001 in 54 of 64.
