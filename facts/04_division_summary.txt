Observations
============
- Dhaka division is both the biggest (26.9% of people) and the biggest gainer: its share rose by 4.5 points, the largest shift of any division.
- Barisal is the smallest division and the slowest growing (0.60% per year); its share fell from 7.0% to 5.5%.
- Sylhet and Chattogram grew faster than the national average (1.37% per year) and gained share.
- Only Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet gained share. The other five (Mymensingh, Rangpur, Rajshahi, Khulna, Barisal) lost share:
  their populations grew, but more slowly than the country as a whole. Barisal lost the most (about 1.5 points), then Khulna (1.4).
- Dhaka division is the only one with density above 2,000 people per km2 (2,226); Barisal is the least dense division (705).
