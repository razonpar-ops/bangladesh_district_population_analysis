7. Issues and cautions found
============================
1. PRECISION MISMATCH: all 64 of 64 values in Population_1991 are exact multiples of 1,000
   (rounded to the nearest thousand). The 2001, 2011 and 2022 columns are exact counts
   (multiples of 1,000: 0, 0, 0). 1991 growth rates for small districts carry slightly
   more rounding noise (max error 500 people, about 0.2% of the smallest district).
2. FILE NAME MISLEADS: it says 'city'; the data are whole districts (which include cities AND rural areas).
   'Dhaka' means Dhaka District, not just the metropolitan city.
3. BOUNDARIES ARE FIXED TO TODAY'S MAP: Mymensingh became its own division in 2015 and is shown here for all years.
   Divisions/districts therefore appear to exist in 1991; this is a back-cast, which makes comparisons consistent.
4. 'Established' is not a founding date for the place: 41 of 64 districts show 1984 (a nationwide reorganisation).
   It reflects administrative history, so it is a weak proxy for how old or settled an area is.
5. MIXED NAME SPELLINGS: modern (Cumilla, Chattogram, Barishal) and older (Jessore, Bogra, Jaipurhat) forms are
   mixed, and the division is spelled 'Barisal' while the district is 'Barishal'. Join with 'Abbr.' not names.
6. 'Native' column is Bangla script. Make sure UTF-8 is used when opening the file.
7. Census intervals are 10, 10 and 11 years in this analysis (nominal). Actual census dates are not in the file.
8. The data cannot explain WHY populations changed (migration, births, floods, boundary changes).
